# Closet item review — v0.17

Each of the 69 options was selected through the actual closet UI and reviewed on Stella from the front, profile and back: 207 preview poses across 11 labelled sheets. The capture runs at simulated phone DPR 2 and exercises the high-density 800×960 preview. Review sheets are generated in /tmp by qa/closet-audit.cjs.

Corrections: trousers/leggings fit and side-waist alignment; plain boyshorts construction; consistent hoodie/shorts colors; curved headwear; single-wrist watch; wrist-relative bracelet angle/depth; matching household pose sleeves. White base layers stay opaque. The Underwear tab can inspect its selections while preserving the outer outfit.
