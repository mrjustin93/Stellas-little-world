# Playing Stella’s Little World v0.5

Open the game's HTTPS link and tap Step inside (or Welcome back).

- Drag the joystick or use WASD/arrow keys. Tap Walk to switch to Jog. Movement stays proportional to joystick pressure.
- Approach the Garden mat and Interact to go outside. Approach the front doorstep to return.
- Open the garden gate, then approach an empty bed and Interact to plant a seed. Interact again to water it. After one full game day, the watered plant ripens. Interact to collect three strawberries and one seed.
- Crops never die and need only one watering. They stay ripe until harvested. Time advances only while playing, and pauses in menus/dialogue. Tap the clock to pause or resume it. A full game day takes ten real minutes. There is no offline time penalty.
- The seed basket beside the cabin refills your pouch to at least twelve seeds for free.
- Bag shows seeds, strawberries, meadow flowers and the reusable watering can. Pick flowers in the eastern meadow, and follow the trail south to the picnic blanket.
- Map shows the 840×680 outdoor world. The pink dot marks Stella's location. Use Escape or the close button to return.

Progress saves to this browser/device every five seconds, after actions, when the tab is hidden, and when leaving. It includes location, crops, inventory, clock, pause state, gate and walk/jog preference. The Little moments journal remains saved as before. Browser data clearing or private browsing may remove saves; saves do not sync across devices. The on-screen status tells you if saving is unavailable. Keep the same site address to continue your farm.
